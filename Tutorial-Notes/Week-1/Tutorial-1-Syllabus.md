# Tutorial 1: Introduction to Hardware and Schematic Design

This tutorial introduces the role of a hardware member, the electrical structure of an RDC robot, basic electronic components, and schematic design using KiCad.

## Learning Content

### Hardware in an RDC Robot

- The role and responsibilities of a hardware member.
- Overview of the robot's electrical structure:
  - Battery.
  - Fuse and power distribution.
  - Relay and emergency stop.
  - Motor controllers.
  - Sensors.

### Basic Electronics

- Introduction to passive components:
  - Resistors.
  - Capacitors.
  - Diodes.
- Introduction to power, signal, and communication circuits.
- Introduction to the microcontroller unit (MCU).
- How to read component datasheets.

### KiCad and Schematic Design

- Introduction to KiCad version 10.0.
- Basic schematic symbols.
- Net labels.
- Power symbols.
- Hierarchical schematic organisation.

## Homework 1: Schematic Design

### Task

Design a schematic for a specified section of the RDC main control board.

### Schematic Requirements

Your schematic should include:

- Correct design of IC's peripheral circuit.
- Correct component values.
- Appropriate power connections.
- Clearly labelled input and output signals.
